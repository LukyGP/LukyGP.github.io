LukyGP – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

About the Application

LukyGP is an Android application providing historical motor racing results, driver information and racing statistics.

Personal Information

LukyGP does not require user registration and is not intended to collect personal information from its users.

Application Data

The application downloads historical racing results and driver photographs from Google Drive to display its content.

Google Drive Access

LukyGP Sync is a separate development tool used by the developer to maintain application data stored in Google Drive.

Google Drive authorization is used only for managing the developer's application files. Regular LukyGP users are not required to authorize Google Drive access.

Data Sharing

LukyGP does not intentionally sell or share users' personal information with advertisers.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the LukyGP GitHub profile.
