About LukyGP

LukyGP is an Android application dedicated to the history of motor racing, with a focus on historical Grand Prix competitions and other racing events.

The application presents race results, drivers, racing cars and historical statistics.

Racing History

Explore Grand Prix, Voiturette, Cyclecar, Sports Car and Hill Climb events from the early years of motor racing.

LukyGP is currently under development. The application is not yet publicly available.
